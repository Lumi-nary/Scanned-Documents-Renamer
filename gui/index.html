How Scanned Documents Renamer Works

1. Scan Documents

Scan your paperwork into your scanner's designated output folder (e.g. Temporary/Unprocessed).

2. AI Vision Inspection

The app detects when the scanner finishes writing, reads headers, document numbers, dates, and client identities.

3. Automatic Filing

The file is renamed with a clean, standardized filename and moved automatically into the client's folder.

Supported Document Types

  • Statement of Account (SOA / OPE): Renamed strictly to reference digits (e.g. 13656.pdf, 20220129.pdf).
  • Secretary's Certificates & Corporate Documents: Extracts notarial Doc. No. (e.g. Secretary's Certificate Doc. No. 384.pdf).
  • Certificates of Incorporation & Registration: Reads SEC Reg. No. or BIR OCN.
  • Transmittal Sheets: Formatted with date (e.g. Transmittal Sheet 01_08_2024.pdf).
  • Summary of Services Rendered: Formatted with service period (e.g. Summary of Services May to August 2018.pdf).
Tip for Office Staff: When scanning a stack of papers for a specific client that includes generic receipts, you can set the Active Client Context so all subsequent pages are automatically filed under that client until you click Clear.
